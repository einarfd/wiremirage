# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith, argue about the work rather than the
person, and accept that the maintainer may decline a change without that being
a judgement of you.

## Expected behaviour

- Keep discussion technical and specific. "This breaks X because Y" beats
  "this is wrong".
- Respect that other people's time is finite — including reviewers'. A clear
  reproduction or a focused diff is a courtesy.
- Assume the other person knows something you don't, and ask.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing others' private information,
and unwelcome sexual attention. This applies in issues, pull requests, commit
messages, and any other project space.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer at
[einarfd@mailthief.com](mailto:einarfd@mailthief.com). Reports are handled
privately. The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close issues or pull
requests, and block accounts, at their discretion and without a debate about
whether the line was crossed.

This is a small project without a moderation team; the policy is short on
purpose, and applied with judgement rather than procedure.
